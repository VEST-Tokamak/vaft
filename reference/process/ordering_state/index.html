Overview

The ordering contracts of vaft.validation.orderings read a state as a row keyed by the names in vaft.validation.orderings.ORDERING_QUANTITIES. This module builds those rows from what a reconstructed VEST state actually carries – global equilibrium scalars, Thomson profiles, the plasma-current history – with the vaft.formula kernels the quantity registry names, and with nothing else.

Three builders, one per scope, because a global d_i/a and a profile rho_s/L_Te are different quantities on different scales (#1627):

  • global_ordering_quantities – one number per state, on a or R0;
  • profile_ordering_quantities – one number per flux surface, on that surface’s own gradient length or connection length;
  • time_history_ordering_quantities – one number per state from its time history (evolution rate, age since onset).

ordering_table applies the global and time-history builders row by row to a table of states; ordering_coverage reports which quantities a table could assess, by the data-availability tiers of #1627.

Nothing is imputed. A quantity whose inputs are missing, non-finite or non-positive is NaN, so the contract evaluation reports it UNASSESSED; an ion temperature is never set equal to T_e here, and a density is never filled in from another diagnostic. A caller who adopts such an assumption does it explicitly and records it.

One ion species. The ion mass density is n_i m_i with n_i = n_e (quasineutral, singly charged main ion); Z_eff enters only the resistivity and the electron collisions. Thermal speeds are sqrt(T/m) (NRL formulary), as vaft.formula.ordering uses.

Gradient lengths are |T/(dT/dr)| on the minor-radius coordinate r in metres, from vaft.formula.utils.normalized_gradient_scale_length; a flat or non-monotonic stretch gives a long L and a small ordering, never a sign.

—dict-

lundquist_number, ion_skin_depth_over_a, inverse_aspect_ratio, beta, beta_over_inverse_aspect_ratio, each a float or NaN.

Convention

S is taken on the minor radius a, the scale of vaft.validation.orderings.ORDERING_QUANTITIES; the ion density is n_e (singly charged main ion).

Processing steps · Applicability · Limitations · Provenance

Processing steps

  1. epsilon = a / R0.
  2. With n_e: d_i = c / omega_pi at n_i = n_e and v_A from B0 and n_i m_i.
  3. With T_e, Z_eff and lnLambda as well: Spitzer eta and S = mu0 a v_A / eta.
  4. beta passes through, and beta / epsilon follows.

Applicability

Machine-independent.

Limitations

A single global T_e stands for the whole plasma in eta; the resistivity of a peaked profile varies by an order of magnitude from core to edge, so S is an order-of-magnitude statement.

Provenance

  1. Issue #1627 §1 and §4; the Lundquist number as in D. Biskamp, Magnetic Reconnection in Plasmas, Cambridge (2000), Ch. 1.

state (global and time-history quantities by registry name), points (profile quantities per surface with a rho_tor_norm column, or None) and provenance.

Convention

b0 is the vacuum field scaled to the geometric centre, B0 = b0 r0 / R0; the profile field is B0 R0 / (R0 + r) (vacuum, outboard midplane) and r = rho_tor_norm a (circular approximation). The global n_e and T_e are cross-section means int f rho drho / int rho drho. beta is IMAS beta_tor, a fraction. These are the conventions of the #1629 ordering atlas.

Raises

  • ValueError The ODS has no equilibrium slice within time_tolerance of time, or the slice has no positive minor radius, major radius or vacuum field.
Processing steps · Defaults · Applicability · Limitations · Provenance

Processing steps

  1. Match the equilibrium slice to time by its own time (the IDS time vector fills a slice without one), within the tolerance; never by index. The vacuum b0 is matched on the IDS time vector.
  2. Read a (boundary.minor_radius, else half the outline’s radial width), R0 (boundary.geometric_axis.r, else the outline’s radial centre), the vacuum b0 at that slice scaled to R0, I_p, beta_tor and the q profile.
  3. dI_p/dt from the equilibrium I_p history, numpy.gradient(I_p, t) at the slice.
  4. Match the core_profiles slice to the equilibrium time within the tolerance; area-mean n_e and T_e for the global quantities.
  5. global_ordering_quantities and time_history_ordering_quantities give state; profile_ordering_quantities on r = rho_tor_norm a gives points.

Defaults

time_tolerance = 0.5 ms is a validated-workflow default, the pairing tolerance of vaft.process.transport_state. use_ion_temperature = False is a conventional choice: no ion temperature is assumed.

Applicability

Machine-independent.

Limitations

Mode and layer quantities (k_perp_rho_i, ion_skin_depth_over_layer, …) need mode numbers or a layer width the ODS does not carry; the caller adds them to state. The circular r overstates r in an elongated plasma by up to sqrt(kappa), which shortens L_T in metres by the same factor.

Provenance

  1. Issue #1823 (advisory ordering check in adapter run provenance) and its follow-up proposal: one builder from a stage’s ODS to the ordering_state mapping, matched by time.

Convention

rho_i uses sqrt(T_i/m_i) (NRL), the convention of vaft.formula.ordering; sqrt(2T/m) would raise it by sqrt 2. The Debye length is taken against L_Te, the profile’s own scale.

Raises

  • ValueError The profile arrays do not share one length, or r is not increasing.
Processing steps · Applicability · Limitations · Provenance

Processing steps

  1. Gradient lengths L_T = |T / (dT/dr)| per species on r, on the finite positive samples of that species’ temperature only.
  2. rho_s from T_e and rho_i = v_ti / Omega_ci with v_ti = sqrt(T_i/m_i); each over its own L_T.
  3. Braginskii collision times, mean free paths v_t tau and the parallel Knudsen numbers on qR; magnetizations |Omega_c| tau.
  4. Sauter collisionalities with the local epsilon = r / R.
  5. lambda_D / L_Te.

Applicability

Machine-independent.

Limitations

Gradient lengths of sparse Thomson channels are finite differences of a few points; a profile fit should be passed when the raw channels are noisy. One main-ion species with n_i = n_e (no impurity dilution); impurities enter only through Z_eff in the electron collisions. One Coulomb logarithm serves both species, so the ion collision time and Sauter’s nu*_i use the caller’s (electron) lnLambda rather than Sauter’s own ion value – a 10-20 % effect on the ion quantities.

Provenance

  1. Issue #1627 §6-§8 and the registry vaft.validation.orderings.ORDERING_QUANTITIES; collisionality as in O. Sauter, C. Angioni and Y. R. Lin-Liu, Phys. Plasmas 6, 2834 (1999).

—dict-

tau_evolution_over_tau_alfven and tau_age_over_tau_resistive, each a float or NaN.

Convention

The evolution time is the plasma current’s; another state variable (stored energy, axis position) gives another tau_evol and is the caller’s to pass. Both ratios use the minor radius a.

Processing steps · Applicability · Limitations · Provenance

Processing steps

  1. tau_A = a / v_A from B0 and n_i m_i (n_i = n_e).
  2. tau_evol = |I_p / (dI_p/dt)|; a zero rate leaves it NaN rather than infinite.
  3. tau_R = mu0 a^2 / eta with Spitzer eta, and the age over it.

Applicability

Machine-independent.

Limitations

tau_R uses one global T_e, as in global_ordering_quantities; the current-relaxation statement is order-of-magnitude.

Provenance

  1. Issue #1627 §9 (quasi-static equilibrium) and §10 (resistive current relaxation).
