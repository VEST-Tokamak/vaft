Overview

The coordinate is psi_N = rho_pol**2, never rho_tor. Finite dF/dpsi_N therefore implies dP/drho_pol(0)=0, without constraining dF/dpsi_N(0). The legacy utils.fit_profile interface and all its modes are unchanged.

evaluate_regularized_profile

source 
evaluate_regularized_profile(psi_n, parameters, config, *, derivative=0, allow_extrapolation=False)
Show source vaft/formula/regularized_profiles.py, lines 119–164

Evaluate a positive regular flux profile or its flux derivative.

\[F(x)=s\sum_j B_j(x/d)\,[f+\log(1+\exp(\theta_j))]\]

Parameter order and basis [1] are shared with the offline fit. Finite F’ implies dF(rho_pol squared)/drho_pol=0 on axis without fixing F’(0).

Limitations

Outside the declared domain the default is NaN. Explicit extrapolation carries no positivity guarantee. Numerical overflow is not clipped.

fit_regularized_profile

source 
fit_regularized_profile(psi_n, values, *, config=None)

Fit independent samples with Huber residuals and quadratic curvature.

\[J=\frac{1}{N}\sum_i H_\delta((F(x_i)-y_i)/s) +\frac{\lambda}{2}\int_0^1 [d^2(F/s)/du^2]^2\,du\]

The Huber term [1] is quadratic within delta and linear outside it; the curvature term remains separately quadratic in u=psi_N/domain_max. Reuses VAFT’s legacy free-polynomial fit for initialization and SciPy’s least-squares kernel. No boundary anchor or post-fit clipping is applied.

Limitations · References

Limitations

Sigma weighting is not implemented. Scale and robust weights are numerical assumptions, never diagnostic sigma. Invalid inputs are rejected, not dropped. Callers own independent masks, branch grouping and support.

References

  1. P. J. Huber, Robust Estimation of a Location Parameter, Annals of Mathematical Statistics 35 (1964), 73-101.

profile_basis

source 
profile_basis(psi_n, config, derivative=0)
Show source vaft/formula/regularized_profiles.py, lines 69–116

Evaluate the positive profile basis or its flux-coordinate derivatives.

\[B_j(u)={n\choose j}u^j(1-u)^{n-j},\qquad u=\psi_N/d\]

The spline alternative uses six clamped cubic B-splines [1]. Ordering is ascending Bernstein index or spline index; derivatives are in psi_N.

Limitations

Extrapolated basis values are mathematical evaluations only. Positive partition-of-unity controls guarantee positivity only on the declared domain.
