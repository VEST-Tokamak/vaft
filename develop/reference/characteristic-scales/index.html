Characteristic scales and orderings

At a glance

Architecture position
The conceptual bridge from a plasma state to its characteristic frequencies, times and lengths, from ratios of those scales to asymptotic orderings, and from orderings to reduced models, applicability and resolvability - with the VAFT function that computes each scale today.
Prerequisites / inputs
None. The Asymptotic orderings and Plasma models pages go deeper on the orderings and on the models.
Expected result
Which kind of quantity a number is (scale, ordering, regime, similarity coordinate, applicability verdict or resolution), which comparison scale an ordering must name, and where each one is computed.
Status
Maintained against the pinned develop baseline.

This page answers one question: how does a plasma state become a statement about which model may describe it? VAFT spreads the answer over several layers. This page names the steps and the vocabulary that keeps them apart (issue #1724), and links to where each step is implemented.

physical plasma state                    n, T, B, q, a, R, I_p(t), ...
        ↓
dimensional characteristic scales        Ω_ci, ρ_i, d_i, λ_mfp, L_T, τ_A, τ_R, ...
        ↓
ratios of scales                         ρ_i/L_T, d_i/a, λ_e/qR, τ_evol/τ_A, ...
        ↓
asymptotic orderings                     ρ_i/L_T ≪ 1, S ≫ 1, ...
        ↓
reduced physical models                  ideal MHD, drift kinetics, gyrokinetics, ...
        ↓
state-specific applicability             SUPPORTED / OUTSIDE / UNASSESSED / NOT_APPLICABLE
        ↓
diagnostic / numerical resolvability     is the structure resolved at all?

Each arrow is a separate step, and none of them is implied by the one before.

step owned by page
scales vaft.formula (domain modules; a canonical scale layer is planned in #1064) this page, Formula reference
ratios and orderings vaft.formula.ordering, vaft.validation.orderings (#1627) Asymptotic orderings
models vaft.code.formalism, vaft.validation.model_contracts (#1727, #1823) Plasma models
applicability vaft.validation.applicability, vaft.process.ordering_state (#1639, #1628, #1627 §2) Credibility and applicability
resolvability not implemented below

Four different kinds of statement

kind what it is example what it does not say
characteristic scale a dimensional quantity of the state $\rho_i$, $d_i$, $\lambda_{\rm mfp}$, $L_T$, $\tau_A$, $\tau_R$, $\Omega_{ci}$ anything about an approximation
ordering parameter a dimensionless ratio of two named scales $\rho_i/L_T$, $d_i/a$, $\lambda_e/qR$, $\omega/\Omega_{ci}$, $\tau_A/\tau_R$ which model is valid
physical regime a qualitative name for where one or more orderings sit banana / plateau / Pfirsch–Schlüter; ideal / resistive; strongly / weakly magnetized an exact boundary, unless a theory defines one
model applicability whether the assumptions of one formula, approximation or solver are supported by one state evaluate_contract(contract("ideal_single_fluid_mhd"), state) that the model’s output is correct

A characteristic scale on its own establishes nothing. $\rho_i = 3$ mm is neither small nor large until it is divided by something, and the answer changes with the divisor (§ Name the comparison scale). A regime name is a reading of orderings. The banana regime is $\nu_* \ll 1$, and the plateau regime has no ordering of its own (Asymptotic orderings, “Ordering contracts”). The neoclassical and NTV regime diagrams of #1111 (ntv_precession_regimes and the collisionality-regime figures in Scientific diagrams) draw regimes the same way: as readings of ratios, with boundaries only where the theory gives one [7]. Applicability is a verdict on a state and belongs to the validation layer. It is never a property of the ordering’s definition.

What exists today

The page distinguishes three states, and says which applies everywhere it names an interface:

  • implemented on develop. A function name in code is importable from develop.
  • planned under an open issue. Named with its issue; no interface is promised.
  • open PR only. None at the time of writing. Interfaces in open PRs are not documented here as stable.

The main gap is #1064 (open): a canonical scale layer (vaft.formula.scales) does not exist yet. Today each scale lives in the domain module that first needed it, listed below. When #1064 lands, the tables below should point at it and the domain functions stay as convention-bearing variants.

Scale taxonomy, by physical role

Scales are grouped by what they mean physically, not by the module that computes them. All functions are in vaft.formula unless the path says otherwise. Units are SI, temperatures in eV.

Frequencies and rates

scale meaning VAFT function returns
$\omega_{ps}$ plasma frequency of species $s$ [1] waves.plasma_frequency(n, q, m) rad/s
$\Omega_{cs}$ gyrofrequency, signed with the charge particle.gyrofrequency(q, m, B) rad/s
$\tau_e$, $\tau_i$ Braginskii collision times [2] ordering.braginskii_electron_collision_time, ordering.braginskii_ion_collision_time s
$\tau_e$, $\tau_i$ (several ion species) Braginskii, multi-species kinetic.electron_collision_time_from_T_e_n_species, kinetic.ion_collision_time_from_T_i_n_species s
$\omega_t$ transit frequency $v/qR_0$ neoclassical.transit_frequency(v, q, R0) rad/s
$\omega_b$ deeply trapped bounce frequency neoclassical.deeply_trapped_bounce_frequency(v_perp, q, R0, epsilon) rad/s
$\nu_{\rm eff}$ effective trapped-particle collision frequency $\nu/\epsilon$ neoclassical.trapped_particle_effective_collision_frequency 1/s
$\omega_A$ shear-Alfvén frequency $k_\parallel v_A$ stability.shear_alfven_frequency(k_parallel, v_A) rad/s

Not implemented:

  • a direct NRL $\nu_{ei}$ or $\nu_{ee}$ frequency function. Use the inverse of a collision time, and say so.
  • magnetic-drift precession $\omega_D$ and $E\times B$ precession $\omega_E$ as computed quantities. They are inputs [rad/s] to ntv.ntv_precession_frequency and particle.bounce_harmonic_detuning. The drift velocities (particle.exb_drift_velocity, grad_b_drift_velocity, curvature_drift_velocity) are velocities, not frequencies. vaft.code.transp.profiles.exb_frequency reads $\omega_E$ from TRANSP output.

Angular frequency, ordinary frequency and inverse time are different quantities.

quantity unit relation
angular frequency $\omega$ rad/s $\omega = 2\pi f$
ordinary frequency $f$ Hz what a spectrum or a DAQ rate is quoted in
inverse time $1/\tau$ 1/s a rate; carries no phase

Every angular-frequency function above returns rad/s; the collision times are in s and $\nu_{\rm eff}$ in 1/s. At $B = 0.1$ T, $\Omega_{ce} \approx 1.76\times10^{10}$ rad/s is $f_{ce} \approx 2.8$ GHz. Comparing $\Omega_{ce}$ with a 2.8 GHz bandwidth without the $2\pi$ is an error of 6.3. A period is $2\pi/\omega$, not $1/\omega$: $1/\Omega$ is the time to turn one radian, and that is what timescale_hierarchy plots as the gyration time.

Speeds

scale VAFT function
thermal speed $v_t = \sqrt{T/m}$ (NRL convention, no factor 2) ordering.thermal_speed(T, m)
ion sound speed $c_s$ sol.ion_sound_speed(T_e, T_i, m_i, Z, gamma_i)
Alfvén speed $v_A$ stability.v_alfven_from_B_n_mi(B, n, m_i)

Microscopic lengths

scale physical meaning VAFT function
$\lambda_D$ Debye screening ordering.debye_length(n_e, T_e) (electron)
$\rho_e$, $\rho_i$ gyro-orbit, finite-Larmor-radius scale particle.larmor_radius(q, m, v_perp, B)
$\rho_s$ sound gyroradius, the drift-wave scale ordering.sound_gyroradius(T_e, m_i, B, Z)
$d_e$ electron inertia scale ordering.inertial_length(n, m, q) with the electron mass
$d_i$ ion inertia, Hall scale ordering.inertial_length(n, m, q) with the ion mass
$\lambda_{\rm mfp}$ mean free path $v_t\tau$ ordering.mean_free_path(v_t, tau)

These are not universally ordered by magnitude. With $v_t=\sqrt{T/m}$, $d_i/\rho_i = \sqrt{2/\beta_i}$: in a low-$\beta$ tokamak $d_i$ exceeds $\rho_i$, by a factor that depends on the state, and the two meet only at $\beta_i\sim1$. $d_e/\rho_e$ is $\sqrt{2/\beta_e}$ in the same way. $\lambda_D$ is the smallest in a fusion plasma, but not by a fixed factor.

Orbit and parallel-motion scales

scale VAFT function assumption
banana width $\Delta_b \approx q\rho/\sqrt\epsilon$ [6, 7] neoclassical.banana_width(rho, q, epsilon) large aspect ratio, deeply trapped
transit and bounce frequency above circular large-aspect-ratio flux surfaces
connection length $qR$ no analytic function; field-line maps in vaft.process.equilibrium.connection_length_map and vaft.process.field_line_topology.connection_length_weight —

The analytic orbit scales carry the large-aspect-ratio ordering $\epsilon \ll 1$. In VEST $\epsilon \approx 0.7$ (the #1629 atlas median), so they are order-of-magnitude estimates there, not results.

Fluctuation and layer lengths

scale meaning where it comes from
$1/k_\perp$, $1/k_\parallel$ perpendicular and parallel wavelength of a perturbation the caller: mode numbers, a $k_y$ grid, a measured spectrum
$\delta_{\rm layer}$ width of a resonant (tearing, resistive) layer the caller, or a scaling estimate such as $a S^{-2/5}$ [4]

Neither is a property of the equilibrium state alone, so VAFT has no function that returns them. The mode-scope and layer-scope ordering quantities take them as inputs and are UNASSESSED without them.

Profile and equilibrium scales

\[L_n=-\left(\frac{d\ln n}{dr}\right)^{-1},\qquad L_T=-\left(\frac{d\ln T}{dr}\right)^{-1},\qquad L_p=-\left(\frac{d\ln p}{dr}\right)^{-1}.\]

These need three choices before they are numbers, and VAFT keeps them apart (#551 and #563, closed):

concept what it fixes VAFT name
profile coordinate the grid the profile is given on ($\rho_{\rm tor,N}$, $\psi_N$, $R$, …) profile_coordinate
gradient coordinate what $r$ in $d/dr$ is ($r_{\rm minor}$, $\rho$, $\psi$, …) gradient_coordinate
reference length what a gradient is normalized by: $a/L$, $R/L$, or none reference_length (a_minor, R_major_axis, R_major_surface, L_ref, "none")

vaft.process.profile_gradients.profile_gradient takes all three explicitly, and its convention presets (tglf, cgyro, gs2, gkw, gene) fix them the way each code does. The table of combinations is in Equilibrium representations, “Profile gradients”. The pure kernel is utils.normalized_gradient_scale_length(x, y, a), which returns $a/L_y$. There is no bare L_n/L_T function on purpose: a gradient length without its coordinate is ambiguous.

Other equilibrium scales: minor radius $a$ and major radius $R_0$ with equilibrium.inverse_aspect_ratio_from_a_R; the magnetic shear length geometry.shear_length_from_q_R0_s (shear itself from equilibrium.shear_from_r_q).

These are different physical constructions. One state can have several “times” with the same name, and they must not be swapped:

construction example note
oscillation period $2\pi/\omega$ includes the $2\pi$
inverse rate $1/\nu$, $1/\Omega$ one radian or one e-fold, not one cycle
crossing time $\tau_A$, $\tau_s$ depends on the length it crosses
diffusion time $\tau_R$, $\tau_D$ $\propto L^2$; the slowest cylindrical mode of $\tau_R$ decays $j_{01}^2\approx5.8$ times faster
confinement time $\tau_E$ a ratio of an inventory to a loss, not a microscopic rate
growth or damping time $1/\gamma$ a property of a mode, not of the state alone
plasma age, pulse length $\tau_{\rm age}$, $\tau_{\rm pulse}$ a property of the discharge history
measurement cadence EFIT slice spacing not a physical scale at all (below)

Scale classes are not a magnitude ordering

A schematic ladder is useful for orientation:

lengths:  Debye → gyro / inertial → orbit / turbulence → profile gradient → minor radius → major radius
times:    gyro motion → orbit / collisions → Alfvénic → mode / wall response → transport → current diffusion → discharge evolution

These are scale classes, not a universal magnitude ordering. The actual order is a property of one state and must be computed from it. vaft.diagram.timescale_hierarchy does exactly that: it evaluates each time from a formula kernel for one illustrative low-field state (only the pulse length is an input) and places it by value, so the hierarchy is a result, not an assumption (Asymptotic orderings, “A worked hierarchy”).

The ladder fails in practice:

  • In a low-field device the electron transit frequency can reach the ion gyrofrequency. In VEST, $\omega_{t,e}\approx5\times10^6$ against $\Omega_{ci}\approx10^7$ rad/s (Plasma models §7).
  • A short pulse compresses the slow end. In the #1629 atlas the median $\tau_{\rm age}/\tau_R$ is 0.57: current diffusion is not slower than the discharge, so “discharge evolution” is not the last rung.

Name the comparison scale

L is part of an ordering’s definition. It is never universal. The same numerator over different denominators answers different questions:

ratio question VAFT registry name
$d_i/a$ is the Hall term negligible on the scale of the device? ion_skin_depth_over_a (global)
$d_i/L_T$ is it negligible on the scale of the temperature profile? not registered
$d_i/\delta_{\rm layer}$ is it negligible inside a resonant layer? ion_skin_depth_over_layer (layer)
$\rho_i/a$ where is the device in similarity space? not an ordering: $\rho_*$ (next section)
$\rho_i/L_{T_i}$ is the FLR correction to the background small? rho_i_over_LTi (profile)
$k_\perp\rho_i$ is a fluctuation larger than a gyro-orbit? k_perp_rho_i (mode)

Every ordering quantity in vaft.validation.orderings.ORDERING_QUANTITIES records its comparison scale explicitly. The issue’s proposed vocabulary maps onto those fields:

concept where VAFT records it
scale value the kernel’s return value (OrderingQuantity.kernels names the kernels)
scale kind OrderingQuantity.definition, e.g. "d_i / a"
reference scale OrderingQuantity.scale, e.g. "a", "q R", "delta_layer", "1 / k_perp"
scope OrderingQuantity.scope: global, profile, mode, local, time_history
group OrderingQuantity.group: foundational, global, profile, perturbation, layer, time_history
species in the name: electron_..., ion_..., rho_s (electron temperature, ion mass)
radial location a column of the point table built by vaft.process.ordering_state.profile_ordering_quantities
mode / event the caller’s input (mode numbers, $k_y$ grid, layer width); missing gives UNASSESSED
provenance the atlas manifest (#1629) and the run provenance of each adapter (#1823)

The kernels in vaft.formula.ordering take the length as an explicit argument for the same reason. A global length cannot be silently swapped for a layer length.

Similarity coordinates are not orderings

Some dimensionless numbers place a state in similarity space. Others are the small or large parameters a derivation expands in. Some symbols appear in both lists, but the role decides what a value means.

role quantities answers used by
similarity coordinate [11, 12] $\rho_$, $\beta$, $\nu_$, $q$, $\epsilon$, $\kappa$ is this state comparable with another machine’s? confinement scalings, operational space
asymptotic ordering $\rho_i/L_T$, $d_i/a$, $\lambda/qR$, $k_\perp\rho_i$, $S$ may this reduced model be used here? ordering contracts, applicability

Concretely:

rho_i / a      → machine similarity / finite-size coordinate     (ρ*: equilibrium.rho_star_from_M_T_B_R_epsilon, on a = R_geo ε)
rho_i / L_T    → local FLR / background-scale ordering            (rho_i_over_LTi)
k_perp rho_i   → fluctuation-scale ordering                       (k_perp_rho_i)

The same function can serve both roles. $\epsilon = a/R_0$ is a shape coordinate in a confinement fit and the expansion parameter of the large-aspect-ratio ordering. Sauter’s $\nu_{*e}$ is a regime coordinate in a neoclassical fit and the ordering of the banana-regime contract. The number is identical; the claim is not.

Similarity coordinates in VAFT:

  • $\rho_*$: equilibrium.rho_star_from_M_T_B_R_epsilon (Verdoolaege form, on $a$), equilibrium.normalized_larmor_radius_from_M_T_a_Bt ($\rho_i/a$);
  • $\nu_*$: neoclassical.electron_collisionality_sauter, neoclassical.ion_collisionality_sauter, equilibrium.nu_star_from_n_T_B_R_epsilon_kappa_I (Verdoolaege form);
  • $\beta$, $\beta_N$, $q$: equilibrium.beta_t_from_n_T_B, stability.beta_N_from_beta_a_B0_Ip, equilibrium.q_cyl_from_B_R_epsilon_kappa_I.

Different $\nu_$ definitions [8, 12] differ by order-unity factors. Never compare two without naming both. vaft.process.confinement.dimensionless_confinement_indices turns fitted engineering exponents into $\rho_$, $\beta$, $\nu_*$, $q$ exponents; its docstring says they are not measured dimensionless dependences.

Geometry is not an ordering

Slab, cylindrical and toroidal describe geometry [3]. Local or global, $a/R\ll1$, $\rho/L\ll1$, $k_\parallel/k_\perp\ll1$ and $\omega/\Omega\ll1$ describe scale separation. They are two axes, not one accuracy ladder. “Large aspect ratio” is an ordering, not a geometry, and a local slab may keep coefficients inherited from a realistic toroidal equilibrium (#1062). The full treatment, with geometry_ordering_map, is Geometric approximations, “Two axes, not one hierarchy”.

From orderings to models

Each plasma description is defined by the orderings it adopts and the ones it relaxes. It is not a rung on one fidelity ladder. Plasma models is the reference:

  • §1 explains why a single “fidelity” axis fails;
  • §2 derives the fluid hierarchy (ideal, resistive, Hall/two-fluid, reduced MHD [5]), drift kinetics, gyrokinetics, particle orbits and kinetic-MHD hybrids;
  • §6 says what “kinetic” means in each VAFT name;
  • §7 is the compact table of what each family removes and which ordering justifies it.

The comparison axes are:

ρ/L    d_i/L    ω/Ω_i    k⊥ρ_i    k∥/k⊥    collisionality    resistivity    locality    distribution-function dynamics

The ordering contracts of #1627 encode them per model (vaft.validation.orderings.CONTRACTS, drawn as ordering_contract_map). The adapter modes of #1823 say which contracts each VAFT code mode consumes, and in which role (governing, outer_solution, layer_solution, fluid_part, kinetic_response, closure, inherited, transport_coupling). That is vaft.validation.model_contracts.ADAPTER_MODES, drawn as adapter_contract_map (Asymptotic orderings, “Which codes consume which contracts”).

Drift kinetics and gyrokinetics are not one ladder

A guiding-centre or drift-kinetic description assumes fields that vary slowly compared with gyromotion, $\rho/L\ll1$ and $\omega/\Omega\ll1$, on every scale it keeps [6]. A gyrokinetic description keeps the low frequency ordering but allows perpendicular structure at the gyroradius, $k_\perp\rho_i\sim O(1)$, at the cost of ordering the fluctuation amplitude and $k_\parallel/k_\perp$ small [9, 14]. So:

drift kinetic   ≠  a lower-fidelity gyrokinetic model
gyrokinetic     ≠  "more kinetic" MHD

They keep different scale information for different problems. Drift kinetics is the natural description of equilibrium-scale kinetic response (neoclassical transport, NTV, kinetic stabilization). Gyrokinetics is the description of $\rho$-scale turbulence, with transport on a separate, slower scale [10]. Plasma models §2.2–§2.3; the gyrokinetic framework is #1353 (open).

Kinetic GPEC is not on the gyrokinetic branch

Kinetic DCON/GPEC and PENTRC are not on the same branch as CGYRO. Both evolve a gyro-averaged kinetic equation under $\omega/\Omega\ll1$, and gyrokinetics reduces to drift kinetics at long wavelength, but they answer different questions:

MHD perturbed equilibrium
  + kinetic orbit / distribution response
  + bounce / transit / drift resonances
        ↓
kinetic correction to the global plasma response            (DCON kinetic, GPEC kinetic, PENTRC) [15-17]

gyrokinetic microturbulence
        ↓
turbulent transport                                          (CGYRO, TGLF)

They differ on every axis that matters: physical formalism (bounce-harmonic drift kinetics against $\delta f$ gyrokinetics), spatial domain (global against flux-tube local), frequency ordering (precession and bounce resonances against drift-wave frequencies) and the problem solved (response or stability against turbulent flux). vaft.code.formalism.PlasmaFormalism records these as separate fields for exactly this reason (Plasma models §3, §4, §11).

From orderings to applicability

An applicability verdict is assembled from separate assumptions, each with its own evidence. The ideal_single_fluid_mhd contract needs, among others,

\[S\gg1,\qquad d_i/a\ll1,\qquad \rho_i/L_{T_i}\ll1,\qquad \omega/\Omega_{ci}\ll1,\]

and these fail independently. Slow evolution, $\tau_{\rm evol}/\tau_A\gg1$, is not one of them: ideal MHD describes Alfvénic dynamics, $\omega\tau_A\sim1$. Slow evolution is the separate quasi_static_equilibrium contract, which an equilibrium sequence consumes. VAFT never reduces them to ideal_mhd_valid = True. vaft.validation.applicability.evaluate_contract returns a margin and a status for every assumption, names the limiting one, and reports a missing quantity as UNASSESSED. Local gyrokinetic applicability likewise involves $\rho_s/L_T$, $\rho_s/L_n$ and $\rho_s$ over the geometric scale, plus fluctuation orderings where a spectrum is available.

This page defines no verdicts. The evaluation, its statuses and its margins are Credibility and applicability. The state tables are built by vaft.process.ordering_state. The VEST population study is the notebook vest_ordering_and_model_applicability.ipynb (#1629).

One model, two scales

A model can be asymptotically appropriate at one scale and inadequate at another. Tearing is the standard example. Take the #1629 atlas medians for VEST: $d_i/a\approx0.32$ and $S\approx1\times10^5$.

  • Globally, $d_i/a\approx0.3$ is below one. The Hall term is not negligible, but single-fluid MHD is not obviously wrong on the device scale.
  • The resistive layer is much thinner than $a$. Its width scales as $\delta/a\propto S^{-2/5}$ for the Furth–Killeen–Rosenbluth tearing layer [4] and $S^{-1/3}$ for the resistive internal kink [13], so $\delta/a\sim0.01$–$0.02$ here, up to factors of mode number and shear.
  • Inside the layer, $d_i/\delta\sim15$–$30$. The two-fluid ordering fails by more than a decade. A single-fluid resistive layer model is outside its contract, even though the device-scale ordering looked acceptable.

The same holds for resistivity: $S_{\rm global}\gg1$ does not make resistivity negligible in the layer, because the layer is where it is not. This is why VAFT registers layer quantities (ion_skin_depth_over_layer, electron_skin_depth_over_layer, rho_s_over_layer) separately from the global ones, and why the RDCON/RMATCH adapter mode consumes resistive_mhd, which orders $d_i/\delta$ and $\rho_s/\delta$, in the layer_solution role, next to ideal_single_fluid_mhd in the outer_solution role.

Physical scales are not resolution

Whether a structure is resolved is a different question from how big it is. A diagnostic cadence or a grid spacing is never a characteristic scale of the plasma, and this page does not define one.

resolution temporal examples spatial examples
diagnostic DAQ sample period, Mirnov bandwidth, fast-camera frame interval, Thomson repetition period chord spacing, optical resolution, tomographic resolution
reconstruction EFIT cadence equilibrium grid spacing
numerical time step, output cadence finite-element mesh, gyrokinetic radial and perpendicular resolution
control control-system update period —

Comparing the two belongs to an analysis layer above both, for example “is the EFIT cadence shorter than $\tau_{\rm evol}$?” or “does the Thomson chord spacing resolve $L_{T_e}$?”. Not implemented. Only the time-grid description exists today (vaft.process.signal_processing.describe_time_grid). Nothing compares a cadence or a grid with a physical scale yet. When it is built, it is evidence on the numerical or transformation axis of the credibility vocabulary, not a new kind of scale.

Diagram contracts

Four diagrams make the chain visible. Two exist. All of them follow the rule of this page: a magnitude order is drawn only when it comes from values.

diagram kind contract state
characteristic_scale_map conceptual, state-independent primitive state → frequencies, speeds, lengths → times → dimensionless ratios → model orderings; no numerical ranking planned
length_scale_ladder state-dependent, or labelled illustrative $\lambda_D$, $\rho_e$, $d_e$, $\rho_i$/$\rho_s$, $d_i$, $\lambda_{\rm mfp}$, orbit width, gradient length, $a$, $R$; placed by value, never in a hard-coded order partly exists as spatial_scale_orderings (#1739: $\rho_e$, $\rho_i$, $\rho_s$, $d_i$, banana width, gradient length, $a$, $R_0$, $qR_0$); $\lambda_D$, $d_e$ and $\lambda_{\rm mfp}$ still planned

model_ordering_map multidimensional relationship map fluid ↔ kinetic closure, ideal ↔ dissipative, single- ↔ two-fluid, long wavelength ↔ finite $k_\perp\rho$, local ↔ global, equilibrium ↔ fluctuation scale; no single fidelity axis exists in parts: ordering_contract_map, adapter_contract_map, reduced_representation_hierarchy, geometry_ordering_map

Diagrams live in vaft.diagram and are conceptual. Ladders of measured states belong to vaft.plot, with the values from vaft.process. The two planned diagrams wait for #1064, so that they draw canonical scale functions rather than define their own.
