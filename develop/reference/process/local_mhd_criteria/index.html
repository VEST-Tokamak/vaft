Overview

Mercier’s ideal-interchange index D_I and the Glasser-Greene-Johnson resistive-interchange index D_R with its geodesic coefficient H, from flux-surface averages of the equilibrium itself – no stability code. They are the quantities DCON and RDCON report as di, dr and h, and the formulas are DCON’s (dcon/mercier.f, mercier_scan) rewritten free of its coordinate normalisation, so the two can be compared surface by surface.

psi(R, Z), F, p, q ─ trace each level ─▶ <1/|grad chi|^2>, <B^2/|grad chi|^2>,
                                         <1/B^2>, <1/(B^2 |grad chi|^2)>, <B^2>, V'
                   ─ GGJ / Mercier ─────▶ D_I, H, D_R = D_I + (H - 1/2)^2

DCON’s sign convention. D_I < 0 is Mercier stable, the opposite sign to vaft.formula.stability.mercier_criterion_circular and the same as vaft.formula.stability.ggj_ideal_interchange_index. With T = 2 pi F p' V' <1/|grad chi|^2> / q'::

D_I = -1/4 + T (1 - T) + p' (V'/q')^2 <B^2/|grad chi|^2>
      * [ p' (<1/B^2> + (2 pi F)^2 <1/(B^2 |grad chi|^2)>) - V''/V' ]
H   = 2 pi F p' V' / q' * ( <1/|grad chi|^2> - <B^2/|grad chi|^2> / <B^2> )
D_R = D_I + (H - 1/2)^2

Orientation. The indices are invariant under the COCOS sign choices, so they are evaluated in DCON’s orientation: chi increases outward, F and q are taken by magnitude, and p' is then negative for a pressure that falls outward.

E and F are not separated. Only E + F enters both indices (D_I = E + F + H - 1/4, D_R = E + F + H^2), so it is reported as one number, E_plus_F.

curvatureSurfaceCurvature-

Per surface the contour, kappa_n, kappa_g [1/m], local_shear [1/m^3], B [T], and the identity residuals in checks.

Convention

n points outward (psi increasing outward), so kappa_n < 0 – the curvature vector pointing inward, as on the outboard side – is the unfavourable sign for a pressure falling outward. kappa_g is along b x n and local_shear is h . curl h in the canonical orientation (B_phi > 0, psi increasing outward); in it closed(local_shear R dl/|grad psi|) = 2 pi d|q|/dpsi, the local shear whose surface average is the global shear.

Raises

  • ValueError Shapes disagree or a level is outside (0, 1).
Processing steps · Applicability · Limitations · Provenance

Processing steps

  1. Orient: psi increasing outward, F by magnitude.
  2. Trace each level (axis-enclosing closed contour) on the refined map.
  3. At the segment midpoints: b, n = grad psi/|grad psi|, kappa = b . grad b (cylindrical, axisymmetric), kappa_n = kappa . n, kappa_g = kappa . (b x n), local_shear = h . curl h with h = B x grad psi / |grad psi|^2; derivatives by central differences of the spline-evaluated fields.
  4. Checks: max |kappa . b|; kappa_n and kappa_g against force balance, (n . grad(B^2/2) + mu0 p' |grad psi|) / B^2 and (b x n) . grad(B^2/2) / B^2; and closed(local_shear R dl/|grad psi|) against 2 pi dq/dpsi from the map.

Applicability

Machine-independent.

Limitations

Only the kappa_n check tests the equilibrium (it assumes ideal static force balance); kappa . b, the kappa_g check and the shear identity hold for any flux map with F = F(psi) and test the numerics. Near the axis the flux gradient vanishes and n is ill-defined; trace from psi_N of about 0.05 on a VEST-sized map.

Provenance

  1. J. M. Greene and J. L. Johnson, Plasma Phys. 10, 729 (1968): local shear h . curl h with h = B x grad psi/|grad psi|^2.
  2. Issue #1902, normal/geodesic curvature and local shear maps.

profileInterchangeProfile-

The indices, E + F, dV/dchi and the five averages per level; an unresolved level is NaN.

Convention

DCON’s: D_I < 0 and D_R < 0 are stable; chi increases outward and F, q enter by magnitude, which leaves the indices unchanged under COCOS sign choices. pressure is in pascal and multiplied by mu0 here.

Raises

  • ValueError Shapes disagree, the levels are not inside (0, 1) and increasing, fewer than five are given, psi_axis equals psi_boundary, or oversample is not positive.
Processing steps · Applicability · Limitations · Provenance

Processing steps

  1. Trace each level and keep the closed contour enclosing the axis.
  2. On it, |grad psi| from a quintic spline of the flux map and B^2 = (|grad psi|^2 + F^2) / R^2; volume-weighted averages.
  3. p' from a cubic spline of the pressure; V'' and (by default) q' on each surface itself, via d/dpsi closed(g dl/|grad psi|) = closed(div(g grad psi / |grad psi|^2) dl/|grad psi|) with g = R and 1/R and second derivatives of the spline, so no difference is taken across levels; all per unit chi.
  4. D_I, H, D_R as in the module notation.

Applicability

Machine-independent.

Limitations

Measured against DCON (mpsi 512, mtheta 1024) with the defaults, D_I on a 0.01 grid of levels: the packaged VEST 39915@319 g-file (129 x 129) within 3.3 % for psi_N 0.05-0.1, 0.5 % for 0.1-0.3 and 0.1 % beyond; the upstream DIII-D-like example (257 x 257) within 0.2 % to psi_N 0.9 and 3.4 % in its steep pedestal (0.95-0.96). With q_from_map=False the VEST core error rises to 19 % (0.05-0.1) and 5 % (0.1-0.3). Near the axis the flux gradient vanishes and every average loses accuracy. DCON clamps p >= 0; this uses the pressure as given. Ideal, static, isotropic pressure; no flow.

Provenance

  1. GPEC dcon/mercier.f mercier_scan.
  2. Glasser, Greene and Johnson, Phys. Fluids 18, 875 (1975).

Processing steps · Applicability · Limitations · Provenance

Processing steps

  1. V', |q| and d|q|/dchi on each surface from the map (mercier_ggj_profile); the enclosed volume from the traced contour by Pappus’ theorem.
  2. s = shear_from_volume and alpha = ballooning_alpha_from_volume (#1720 normalisation) with per-radian flux oriented outward.
  3. The first and second stability boundaries at each s from vaft.formula.stability.s_alpha_marginal_alpha.

Applicability

Machine-independent.

Limitations

The s-alpha model is circular and large-aspect-ratio; on VEST’s aspect ratio alpha_first can be off by a large factor from the true boundary. Inherits the near-axis accuracy of mercier_ggj_profile.
