Overview

Numerics are machine-independent and use SI units. No ODS, solver installation, plotting backend, collisions or transport model is required. Native integration times are preserved; interpolation is only an explicitly recorded comparison or presentation operation. Fields and records live in vaft.data.particle.

analyze_particle_orbit(trajectory, *, bounce_coordinate=None)

Evaluate field, energy, invariants, ordering and defined bounce cycles.

NameTypeUnitDescription
trajectoryParticleTrajectory-

Native computed particle or GC states.

bounce_coordinatecallable, optionalm

Signed field-line arc coordinate s(position) for a known periodic bounce. None leaves action undefined; it is never inferred from an arbitrary 3-D history.
Returns
—dict-

Native-time arrays, dimensional scales, residuals and bounce evidence.

Convention

Full-orbit total energy uses laboratory kinetic energy plus q Phi. GC energy uses p_parallel^2/(2m) + mu B + q Phi. Canonical toroidal momentum uses x p_y - y p_x + q (x A_y - y A_x), in the field’s declared gauge.

Processing steps · Applicability · Limitations · Provenance

Processing steps

Sample fields; resolve parallel and perpendicular energy in the local drift frame; evaluate model-appropriate invariants and dimensionless ordering. Detect turns, then evaluate action only on a supplied periodic arc coordinate whose sampled cycle closes within resolution.

Applicability

Machine-independent. Static fields with adequate numerical resolution.

Limitations

Full-orbit mu is an instantaneous estimate; its gyrovariation is physical. Derivative diagnostics are NaN with an unavailable audit when their stencil crosses the domain boundary; local state diagnostics remain available. Fixed GC mu is not an adiabaticity test. Finite orbit momentum changes under nonaxisymmetry alone do not demonstrate transport or NTV torque.

Provenance

  1. J. R. Cary and A. J. Brizard, Rev. Mod. Phys. 81 (2009) 693.

—ParticleComparison-

Processing steps · Applicability · Limitations · Provenance

Processing steps

Check particle, field and time compatibility; estimate the local full-orbit center in the drift frame; linearly interpolate both positions on a shared physical coordinate. Record initialization and ordering separately.

Applicability

Machine-independent. Matched initial conditions in a controlled GC regime.

Limitations

Local center subtraction is not higher-order gyroaveraging. Separation includes initialization, phase and GC approximation error; numerical and ordering scans are needed to attribute it. A one-gyroperiod time-window average is supplied separately, with incomplete edge windows omitted.

Provenance

  1. F. F. Chen, Introduction to Plasma Physics and Controlled Fusion, 3rd ed. (2016), Sec. 2.2.

Processing steps · Defaults · Applicability · Limitations · Provenance

Processing steps

Build a solenoidal static reference field, initialize a physical particle, apply the documented leading-order GC conversion, and integrate both models to the same physical end time. Pair only their common valid interval.

Defaults

Charge and mass define an illustrative SI particle, not an electron/ion mass ratio. Geometry, speed, pitch and duration are educational benchmark choices; all resolved values are recorded. dt is numerical convenience and must be reduced when charge/mass increases.

Applicability

Machine-independent. Controlled reference experiments, not discharge predictions.

Limitations

Tokamak references use an analytic circular field, not force-balanced VEST equilibrium. Symmetry-breaking output alone establishes no transport rate. Time-varying fields and the second invariant under slow variation are deferred.

Provenance

  1. Particle-motion work packages #2004 through #2009.

Convention

Static SI Cartesian fields; this is a weak-flow, nonrelativistic GC model. Numerical energy conservation is tested, not enforced by projection.

Processing steps · Applicability · Limitations · Provenance

Processing steps

Scale position by a local gyroradius and momentum by characteristic kinetic momentum, integrate the Hamiltonian B-star equations, and preserve accepted states. A field boundary or B-star singularity terminates the trace.

Applicability

Machine-independent. Smooth magnetized static fields, small gyroradius ordering and weak electrostatic drift.

Limitations

DOP853 is not symplectic. No long-time invariant guarantee is claimed. mu conservation is imposed; assess adiabaticity against full Lorentz runs.

Provenance

  1. J. R. Cary and A. J. Brizard, Rev. Mod. Phys. 81 (2009) 693.

Processing steps · Applicability · Limitations · Provenance

Processing steps

Validate initial data, then use drift-kick-drift Boris updates. Check field domains at every sampled position. Preserve only valid completed steps.

Applicability

Machine-independent. Nonrelativistic collisionless motion in static fields.

Limitations

Fixed-step integration must resolve the largest gyrofrequency encountered. A boundary event retains the last valid step, not an inferred wall impact. Steps smaller than the timestamp spacing are rejected; shift t0 toward zero when resolving very short motion at a large absolute time origin.

Provenance

  1. J. P. Boris, Proc. 4th Conf. Numer. Sim. Plasmas, NRL (1970), 3.
